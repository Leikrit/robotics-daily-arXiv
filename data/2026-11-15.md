### [1] [ScrewSense: Mapping the Invisible - Anomaly Detection and Error Classification in Screwdriving Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16789)
*Tobias Lettner,Tatyana Ivanovska,Anwar Al Assadi,Christian Bergler*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本研究利用深度学习对螺丝拧紧过程的多变量时间序列数据进行异常检测与错误分类，异常检测基于VQ-VAE嵌入空间，错误分类评估四种模型架构，准确率最高达100%，并在真实数据上验证了鲁棒性和泛化能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 螺丝拧紧系统产生的过程数据日益增多，准确检测和分类拧紧错误对保证产品质量、避免昂贵返工以及支持产品维护、再利用和回收的可持续性至关重要；传统监测方法（如窗口法或包络曲线）存在局限，机器学习提供了有前景的替代方案。

Method: 数据语料为捕获扭矩、旋转角度等过程参数的多变量时间序列；异常检测方面分析向量量化变分自编码器（VQ-VAE）的嵌入空间；错误分类方面评估四种不同的模型架构。

Result: 异常检测有效，深度学习模型能够区分多种错误类别（超越传统二分类），准确率最高达100%；在真实世界数据上进一步验证了模型鲁棒性。

Conclusion: 结果表明显著的泛化能力，所开发模型在工业螺丝拧紧过程中具有实际应用潜力，可无需专家知识进行错误识别，并显著减少人工工作量和分析时间。

Abstract: The growing volume of process data generated by screwdriving systems, combined with advancements in computing power, data storage, and algorithms, is facilitating the application of artificial intelligence in this field. Accurately detecting and classifying screwdriving errors is crucial for ensuring product quality, avoiding costly rework in manufacturing, and supporting sustainability by facilitating the maintenance, reuse, and recycling of screw-connected products. In this context, machine learning approaches offer a promising alternative to traditional monitoring techniques, such as window-based methods or envelope curves. This study examines the use of deep learning techniques for anomaly detection and error classification in screwdriving processes. The data corpus consists of multivariate time series capturing process parameters, such as torque and rotation angle. For anomaly detection, the embedding space of a vector quantized variational autoencoder was analyzed. In terms of error classification, four different model architectures were evaluated. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of anomaly detection and confirm that deep learning models can distinguish between multiple error categories beyond traditional binary classification. This extends the capabilities of conventional monitoring approaches, achieving accuracies of up to 100%. Additionally, real-world data material was examined in order to further demonstrate model robustness. Overall the results indicate strong generalization capability and highlight the practical potential of the developed models for use in industrial screwdriving processes, eliminating the need for expert knowledge in error identification and significantly reducing manual effort and analysis time.

### [2] [SteinPnP: Non-Parametric Uncertainty Quantification in the Perspective-N-Point Problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15437)
*Baptiste Lardinoit,Jongseok Lee,Andres Yubero,Seong Hun Lee,Javier Civera,Rudolph Triebel*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出SteinPnP，一种基于斯坦变分推断的概率PnP求解器，用非参数粒子集表示完整6自由度位姿后验，解决多模态不确定性问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统优化PnP求解器通常只输出单一位姿，难以表达复杂场景中存在的多模态不确定性；而直接应用斯坦变分推断又容易遗漏候选位姿或赋予错误概率权重。

Method: 利用斯坦变分推断结合粒子表示与优化效率，并引入三项实用改进：发现新位姿、识别不同假设簇、平衡各簇相对概率权重，以更准确捕捉真实后验分布。

Result: 在合成与真实基准上的实验表明，SteinPnP能准确捕捉复杂的多模态不确定性，同时保持有竞争力的位姿估计精度。

Conclusion: SteinPnP有效结合粒子表达力与优化求解效率，并通过针对性改进克服直接应用SVI的缺陷，是一种可靠的6自由度概率PnP求解方案。

Abstract: We propose SteinPnP, a probabilistic Perspective- N-Point (PnP) solver that models the full 6-DoF camera pose posterior using a non-parametric set of pose particles. By lever- aging Stein Variational Inference (SVI), SteinPnP combines the expressivity of particle-based representations with the efficiency of optimization-based solvers. However, a naive application of SVI struggles in the PnP problem, often missing alternative pose hypotheses or assigning incorrect probability weights to them. To address these limitations, we propose practical remedies that discover new potential poses, identify distinct hypothesis clusters, and balance their relative probability weights to capture the true underlying distribution. Our experiments across synthetic and real-world benchmarks demonstrate that SteinPnP accurately captures complex multimodal uncertainties while maintaining competitive accuracy.
