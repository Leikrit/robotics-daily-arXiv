### [1] [Accelerating Safe Reinforcement Learning with Massive Parallelism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08273)
*Joonyoung Lim,Younghwan Yoo*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 针对大规模并行短期rollout与CMDP全回合成本约束不匹配的问题，提出相位聚合成本估计与约束收紧框架，实现可扩展的安全RL。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大规模并行训练提升吞吐与墙钟效率，但其短rollout与CMDP基于全回合成本回报定义的安全约束存在不匹配。

Method: 将错峰环境重置建模为短轨迹段上的相位混合分布，分析相位覆盖带来的偏差，提出从短rollout批次重建回合级成本的相位聚合估计器，并结合有限样本不确定性与分布失配进行约束收紧，兼容on-policy优化。

Result: 基于MJX的Safety Gym实验表明，该方法性能与非大规模并行实现相当，同时显著减少墙钟训练时间。

Conclusion: 相位聚合估计与约束收紧可使安全RL有效适配大规模并行短rollout训练，在保持性能的同时提升训练效率。

Abstract: We investigate the problem of scaling safe reinforcement learning (safe RL) to massively parallel training regimes, where a large number of environments are exe- cuted with short rollout horizons. Although this setting improves data throughput and wall-clock efficiency, it creates a mismatch with constrained Markov decision processes (CMDPs), whose safety constraints are defined over full-episode cost returns. We address this mismatch by analyzing staggered environment resets as a phase-mixture distribution over short trajectory segments. This analysis charac- terizes the bias induced by phase coverage and motivates a phase-aggregated cost estimator that reconstructs episode-level cost estimates from short rollout batches. We further incorporate finite-sample uncertainty and distribution mismatch into a constraint-tightening framework, yielding a scalable Safe RL method compatible with on-policy optimization. Using an MJX-based implementation of Safety Gym, our experiments show that the proposed method achieves performance comparable to non-massively parallel implementations while substantially reducing wall-clock training time.

### [2] [Deformable Contact-Aware 3D Object Placement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08005)
*Songhe Wang,David Miller,Charles A. Kamhoua*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: DCAP将语言引导的3D物体摆放重构为“落下至平衡”过程，通过耦合刚体-可变形模拟器实现接触感知的物理合理摆放。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有语言引导3D摆放方法通常预测刚性6-DoF位姿，忽视室内摆放受接触主导的物理现实，导致预测位姿漂浮、穿透支撑面或无法使软表面形变。

Method: 提出可变形接触感知摆放（DCAP）框架，将摆放重构为落下至平衡过程：不预测静态位姿，而是构建语义释放状态（指定落点、物体尺度与可变形部件），交由耦合刚体-可变形模拟器在重力与摩擦下使物体稳定，从而显式分离语义意图与物理实现。

Result: 在包含2,063个提示-资产-场景案例的基准上，DCAP显著优于静态基线：标准摆放中VLM物理与自然性提升21.2分、人类偏好提升26.5分，平均穿透深度降低72.3%；相比模拟rollout基线，残余速度降低72.1%。

Conclusion: 结果表明，物理准确的3D摆放需要将语义意图与物理实现建模为严格分离的两个过程。

Abstract: Current language-guided 3D object placement typically predicts a rigid 6-DoF pose, ignoring the physical reality that indoor placements are dictated by contact. Consequently, predicted poses often float, interpenetrate supports, or fail to deform soft surfaces. We propose Deformable Contact-Aware Placement (DCAP), a framework that reframes placement as a drop-to-equilibrium process. Rather than predicting a static pose, DCAP constructs a semantic release state that specifies the drop location, object scale, and deformable components, allowing a coupled rigid-deformable simulator to settle the object under gravity and friction. This explicitly separates semantic intent from physical realization. Evaluated on a benchmark of 2,063 prompt-asset-scene cases, DCAP significantly outperforms static baselines. On standard placements, it improves VLM Physics & Naturalness by 21.2 points and human preference by 26.5 points over the strongest raw rigid baseline, while reducing mean penetration depth by 72.3%. Compared to simulated rollout baselines, DCAP reduces residual speed by 72.1%. Ultimately, our results prove that physically accurate 3D placement requires modeling semantic intent and physical realization as strictly separate processes.

### [3] [PVDepth: Panoramic Video Depth Estimation via Geometry-Aware Spatiotemporal Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07873)
*Chuanxin Song,Peixi Peng*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出大规模合成全景视频数据集PanoCARLA及适配的PVDepth端到端深度估计框架，通过球面感知噪声初始化与立方体校正时序建模解决ERP畸变和时序非线性问题，实现几何准确且时序一致的深度序列。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 全景视频深度估计对VR和世界模型很重要，但受限于大规模训练数据稀缺以及ERP投影特有的时空挑战，难以直接迁移透视视频深度模型。

Method: 构建PanoCARLA大规模合成RGB-D全景视频数据集（自然运动轨迹与无人机式漫游视角）；在透视视频深度模型基础上提出PVDepth框架，引入两项机制：渐进式球面感知噪声初始化（将噪声分布从平面退火到球面以适配非均匀信息密度）和立方体校正时序建模（借助辅助立方体贴图时序分支校正ERP域的非线性时序动态）。

Result: 大量实验表明PVDepth性能优越，能生成几何准确且时序一致的深度序列。

Conclusion: PanoCARLA数据集与PVDepth框架有效克服了数据稀缺和ERP投影障碍，为全景视频深度估计提供了可行方案，代码与数据将开源。

Abstract: Panoramic video depth estimation is pivotal for applications such as Virtual Reality and World Models. However, advancements in this field are impeded by two primary obstacles: the scarcity of large-scale training data and the unique spatiotemporal challenges of Equirectangular Projection (ERP), which hinder the direct transfer of perspective models. In this paper, we first present PanoCARLA, a large-scale synthetic RGB-D panoramic video dataset, featuring natural motion trajectories and drone-like roaming perspectives. Building on this foundation, we propose PVDepth, an end-to-end framework adapted from perspective video depth models. To tackle ERP-specific geometric distortions and consequent non-linear temporal dynamics, we introduce two core mechanisms: (1) A Progressive Sphere-aware Noise Initialization strategy that anneals the noise distribution from planar to spherical, guiding the model to adapt to non-uniform information density; and (2) A Cube-rectified Temporal Modeling module that incorporates an auxiliary cubemap temporal branch to rectify non-linear temporal dynamics in the ERP domain. Extensive experiments demonstrate that PVDepth achieves superior performance, generating geometrically accurate and temporally consistent depth sequences.
