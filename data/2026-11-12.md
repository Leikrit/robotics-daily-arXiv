### [1] [Personalized Affective Perception via Continual Learning in Human-Agent Negotiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14247)
*Mehmet Onur Keskin,Berk Buzcu,Nikhil Churamani,Reyhan Aydogan,Hatice Gunes,Catholijn Jonker*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 提出结合持续学习与想象的面部表情识别（CLIFER）的自适应谈判策略，实时个性化效价-唤醒感知，并在人机谈判实验中提升智能体效用与对方让步率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自利智能体与人类谈判时需解读非语言表情并调整行为，但通用面部情感模型在个体和情境间泛化差，存在泛化鸿沟。

Method: 引入CLIFER进行实时效价-唤醒个性化；用差分反馈将效价-唤醒方向与强度变化转化为智能体目标效用调整；采用异步架构在实时交互中后台更新CLIFER；开展66名被试与人形机器人谈判的平衡组内实验。

Result: 持续学习条件智能体个体效用显著更高，参与者让步率更高；平均唤醒估计显著更高且输出范围更广；参与者效用与Nash距离无显著变化。

Conclusion: 将持续情感个性化与差分反馈结合对实时谈判具有实用价值。

Abstract: Self-interested intelligent agents that negotiate with humans should interpret nonverbal expressions and adapt their behavior accordingly. However, generic facial-affect models degrade across individuals and contexts, creating a generalization gap. We introduce an affect-adaptive negotiation strategy that incorporates Continual Learning with Imagination for Facial Expression Recognition (CLIFER) to personalize valence-arousal perception in real time. Differential feedback converts changes in valence-arousal direction and intensity into adjustments of the agent's target utility. An asynchronous architecture updates CLIFER in the background during live interaction. In a counterbalanced within-subject study in which 66 participants negotiated with a humanoid robot, the CL-enabled agent achieved significantly higher individual utility and elicited higher participant concession rates than the FaceChannel baseline. The CL condition yielded significantly higher mean arousal estimates, alongside a broader output range. Participant utility and Nash distance did not change significantly between conditions. These outcomes support the practical value of coupling continual affective personalization with differential feedback for real-time negotiation.

### [2] [Target Selection with a Disappearing-Stimulus Mixed-Reality P300 Interface: Feasibility and Object-Distinguishable Versus Uniform Cues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13906)
*Runqi Pan,Takuto Niinuma,Yunshan Huang,Masato Sugino,Kenta Shimba,Kiyoshi Kotani*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 在头戴式混合现实设备上，P300脑机接口可在刺激消失条件下实现目标选择，但为每个提示赋予稳定的颜色-形状身份并未带来可检测的收益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 混合现实P300接口可直接在用户周围环境中注册可选目的地，但提示在闪烁间隔消失时，用户必须保持一个已不可见的目标。作者检验该接口能否支持目标选择，以及赋予每个提示稳定的颜色-形状身份是否优于视觉上统一的球体。

Method: 6名健康被试在头戴式MR显示的5x5行列任务中，每种提示条件下完成4个试次，采集Fz、Cz、Pz的EEG。采用收缩线性判别分析，并以嵌套验证评估：完整试次从类别平衡、超参数选择和拟合中留出。比较两种提示条件：统一球体与带颜色-形状身份的对象提示。

Conclusion: 消失刺激的MR P300选择在严格试次级评估下可行，但对象身份带来的收益（若有）在该规模试点中过小，无法分辨。

When the cues disappear between flashes, however, the user must retain an intended target that is no longer visible. We tested whether such an interface supports target selection and whether giving each cue a stable colour-shape identity improves it compared with visually uniform spheres. Six healthy adults completed four trials in each cue condition of a 5x5 row/column task on a head-mounted MR display, with EEG from Fz, Cz, and Pz. Shrinkage linear discriminant analysis was evaluated with nested validation in which complete trials were held out from class balancing, hyperparameter selection, and fitting. Both conditions evoked a parietal target-related positivity. The decoded cell was correct in 18 of 48 held-out trials, against 1.9 expected by chance (binomial p < 1e-12), and in three participants individually. Cue format made no detectable difference: complete-target accuracy was 9/24 in both conditions (paired difference 0.0 percentage points, 95% CI [-37, 37]), and held-out AUC was 0.609 for spheres and 0.585 for object cues. Disappearing-stimulus MR P300 selection is therefore feasible under strict trial-level evaluation, but the benefit of object identity, if any, is too small to resolve in a pilot of this size.

### [3] [Audited Gradients, Anchored Policies: Model-Based Reinforcement Learning for Efficient Flow Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13203)
*Ruige Kong,Stefania Fresca*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 提出审计代理模型动作梯度的方法，并据此选择拟合低维反馈律初始化神经策略，在四个算例中以更少高保真求解次数获得不劣于无模型基线的控制器。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高保真求解器评估反馈控制器代价高；可微代理虽降低成本，但预测误差无法检测动作梯度误估这一失效模式，需在优化前识别代理是否可用于策略优化。

Method: 在预留状态上比较代理动作梯度与高保真中心有限差分估计，并按预先设定的接受准则进行审计；通过审计则将低维反馈律拟合到代理动作梯度，否则拟合到求解器估计；该反馈律初始化神经策略，优化时用二次惩罚使策略保持在其附近。

Result: 四个算例中控制器优于或匹配无模型基线，高保真求解器交互次数减少3至156倍；某代理目标预测相关系数0.9996但动作梯度仅0.459，说明预测精度不能识别可用代理；另一代理动作梯度相关0.996，但无锚定惩罚直接优化使目标变差0.52%；通过某固定动作审计的代理在部署控制器实际动作附近未通过，表明通过是局部的。

Conclusion: 代理可用性需通过动作梯度审计判断而非预测精度；动作梯度准确也不足以保证控制器有效；审计结论仅局部有效，需结合锚定惩罚与反馈律初始化来稳健利用代理。

Abstract: Feedback controllers for flows are expensive to design, since each candidate must be evaluated with a high-fidelity solver. Differentiable surrogates reduce that cost, but they also introduce a failure mode that prediction error cannot detect. Policy optimization uses the derivative of the objective with respect to the action, which we call the action gradient and which can be computed either through the surrogate or through the high-fidelity solver. Optimization through the surrogate uses only its action gradient, and a surrogate can follow the flow trajectory closely while misestimating this derivative. The surrogate action gradient is compared against a central finite-difference estimate from the high-fidelity solver on held-out states, under acceptance criteria fixed before the comparison, and we call that comparison an audit. A low-dimensional feedback law is then fitted to the surrogate action gradient when the surrogate passes the audit, and to the solver estimate when it does not. This law initializes a neural policy, and a quadratic penalty holds the policy near it during optimization. The proposed approach is validated on four test cases. The resulting controllers outperform or match a model-free baseline, while using 3 to 156 times fewer high-fidelity solver interactions across the four test cases. One surrogate predicts the objective at correlation 0.9996 but its action gradient at only 0.459, so prediction accuracy does not identify a usable surrogate. Another surrogate, whose action gradient agrees with the solver estimate at correlation 0.996, makes the objective 0.52% worse when the policy is optimized through it without the anchor penalty, so an accurate gradient alone does not provide a working controller. Finally, a surrogate that passes the audit at one fixed action fails it around the actions the deployed controller applies, so a pass is local.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 将TurboReg的兼容图构建重新表述，以严格的Tri-Scale Consistency（TSC）准则取代绝对距离约束；分析表明TSC可同时抑制误匹配外点与坐标畸变外点，并将所得求解器集成到现有流式系统中。

Our analysis shows that TSC suppresses both mismatching and coordinate-distortion outliers. ### [5] [CJRR-Net: A Candidate-Joint Reprojection Ranking for Future-View Selection in Human Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13700)
*Huiting Xi,Marsha Mariya Kappan,Eduardo Benitez Sandoval,Francisco Cruz*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出CJRR-Net，在八相机固定配置下为未来视角选择进行候选关节重投影排序，实现更准确的单视角人体姿态估计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多相机系统在体育与人体运动分析中需要选择信息量最大的视角，以提升人体姿态估计的可靠性，因此研究未来视角选择问题。

Method: 构建候选关节重投影排序网络CJRR-Net，融合当前视角姿态观测、相机配置，以及将估计三维姿态及其不确定性重投影到各候选视角得到的候选特定几何特征，在MPI-INF-3DHP的八个标定相机间进行视角选择。

Result: 所选视角的二维MPJPE为105.18像素，优于共享输入MLP的114.64像素和适配PoseErrNet的117.56像素；在各遮挡分组中平均误差均为所列策略最低；并通过blender rollout展示重复相机选择过程。

We propose the Candidate-Joint Reprojection Ranking Network (CJRR-Net) for future-view selection among eight calibrated cameras in MPI-INF-3DHP. The model combines current-view pose observations, camera configuration, and candidate-specific geometric features obtained by reprojecting the estimated 3D pose and its uncertainty into each candidate view. CJRR-Net achieves a selected-view 2D mean per-joint position error (MPJPE) of 105.18 pixels, compared with 114.64 pixels for the shared-input MLP and 117.56 pixels for adapted PoseErrNet. It also achieves the lowest mean error among the listed policies in each evaluated occlusion group. A blender rollout illustrates repeated camera selection. These findings support candidate-specific geometry for single-view selection in the evaluated fixed eight-camera setting.
