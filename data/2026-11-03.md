### [1] [Proof-Carrying Evolution: Black-Box Search, White-Box Flight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42043)
*Amit Jain,Sergio Sanchez-Hurtado,Victor Rodriguez-Fernandez,Richard Linares*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 该研究检验语言模型能否辅助设计行为可被形式化验证的控制器，提出“证明携带演化”（PCE）框架，在对接限速、机动目标追踪与SO(3)姿态控制三个任务上，比较四种语言模型与经典搜索/综合方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 控制器设计需要可形式化验证的保证，而语言模型生成的设计缺乏可信度；作者希望将语言模型仅用于设计阶段，最终部署的控制律及其证书可被精确检验，从而结合生成能力与形式化可信性。

Method: PCE中，提议者同时给出固定数学形式的控制器及其证书的数值参数，证书检查引导选择，仅返回通过认证的候选；部署物为控制律与证书，语言模型只在设计时使用。在对接限速、机动目标追踪和SO(3)姿态控制上，用配对运行与共享提议预算比较四种语言模型与经典搜索及综合。线性问题在搜索中用精确有理数算术检查证书；SO(3)上先用浮点检查，再对所有报告冠军做精确复核；每个方法最佳运行还接受Lean 4证明。

Result: 线性问题上最强模型在每次运行都胜过标准经典基线，但为同一目标选择证书的经典优化器缩小了大部分差距；所引追踪冠军的排名在从Euler切换到零阶保持动力学后不成立。SO(3)上，与经典变异混合时没有模型可检测地优于CMA-ES；当模型写出全部提议时，一个模型优于CMA-ES，另一个劣于其混合变体；所有搜索中位数仍低于手调律。在对接控制中固定目标与变异算子，保留认证候选规则每次运行都产生新的认证设计，移除该规则则没有。

Conclusion: 语言模型可在设计阶段辅助生成可认证控制器，并在部分线性任务上超过经典基线，但其优势脆弱且依赖证书选择与动力学设定；认证候选保留规则对获得新认证设计至关重要，而整体搜索仍不及手工调参。

Abstract: We study whether a language model can help design controllers whose behavior can be formally checked. In proof-carrying evolution (PCE), a search proposes controllers together with their certificates. Their mathematical forms are fixed; a proposer chooses their numerical parameters together. Certificate checks guide selection, and only a certified candidate can be returned. The deployed artifact is a control law and its certificate; the language model is used only during design. We compare four language models with classical search and synthesis on docking under a speed limit, pursuit of a maneuvering target, and attitude control on SO(3), using paired runs and shared proposal allowances. Certificates are checked in exact rational arithmetic during search on the linear problems. On SO(3), the search uses floating-point checks, followed by exact re-verification of every reported champion. The best run of each method also receives Lean 4 proofs of its stated control properties. On the linear problems, the strongest models beat the standard classical baselines on every run, but a classical optimizer that chooses certificates for the same objective closes most of that lead. Among the cited pursuit champions, the ranking does not survive a change from Euler to zero-order-hold dynamics. On SO(3), no model detectably outperforms CMA-ES when mixed with classical mutation. When models write every proposal, one tested model improves on CMA-ES and the other performs worse than its mixed variant. All search medians remain below the hand-tuned law. In a docking control with the objective and mutation operator held fixed, retaining certified candidates yields new certified designs in every run; removing this rule yields none.

### [2] [BioAgent: Self-Directed Skill Acquisition and Capability Growth for Long-Horizon Laboratory Protocols](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41383)
*Gaojing Zhang,Changwen Chen,Zichen Luo,Jialiang Li,Yuhan Wang,Yifei Xie,Wenzhao Lian*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: BioAgent是一个具身智能体，通过匹配需求与已验证技能、选择工具实现缺失行为并在需要时请求人类教学，结合任务包验证和执行期状态检查，实现实验室协议的自主执行与恢复。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 执行不熟悉的实验室协议时，机器人需识别缺失能力并判断每个操作在实验推进过程中是否仍然有效，即能力充分性与实验有效性的耦合挑战。

Method: 将人工审核的需求匹配到已验证技能和任务包；为缺失行为选择基于工具的实现；学习中需要时请求人类教学；学习到的技能经过顺序任务包验证以检查结果和连续操作兼容性；执行时通过前置条件、结果和实验状态检查控制推进、自主恢复或终止；执行证据指导工具选择，接受的经验支持离线策略更新和任务打包。

Result: 在第二个任务上，BioAgent原样复用17种技能、修改两个现有实现并获取五种新技能类型；在两个双臂实验室任务（分别有20和31个有序技能调用）上，每任务50次运行中完整任务成功率分别为66%和58%并具备自主恢复；25个原子技能各50次自主试验（禁用恢复）成功率84%至100%，其中17个技能达到50/50。

Conclusion: BioAgent能够有效应对实验室协议执行中的能力充分性与实验有效性挑战，通过技能复用、工具选择和人类教学实现对新任务的适应，并在执行中保证可靠推进与恢复。

Abstract: Executing an unfamiliar laboratory protocol requires a robot to identify missing capabilities and determine whether each operation remains valid as the experiment progresses. This letter presents BioAgent, an embodied agent that addresses these coupled challenges of capability sufficiency and experimental validity. It matches human-reviewed requirements to validated skills and task packages, selects tool-based implementations for missing behaviors, and requests human teaching when needed during learning. Learned skills undergo sequential task-package validation to check outcomes and compatibility between successive operations. During execution, precondition, outcome, and experimental-state checks govern progression, autonomous recovery, or termination. Execution evidence guides tool selection, while accepted experience supports offline policy updates and task packaging. On the second task, BioAgent reuses 17 skill types unchanged, revises two existing implementations, and acquires five new types. On two dual-arm laboratory tasks with 20 and 31 ordered skill invocations, BioAgent achieves complete-task success rates of 66\% and 58\% with autonomous recovery over 50 runs per task. Across 25 atomic skills evaluated over 50 autonomous trials each with recovery disabled, success rates range from 84\% to 100\%, with 17 skills achieving 50/50.

### [3] [PGGT-Loc: Primitive-Grounded Geometry Transformer for Feed-Forward Camera Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41833)
*Hanqiao Ye,Yuzhou Liu,Mengqi Rong,Jian Liu,Yangdong Liu,Shuhan Shen*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出PGGT（Primitive-Grounded Geometry Transformer）网络，将单张查询图像直接与平面地图基元对齐，实现前馈式6-DoF相机定位，在稠密表面地图和极简布局模型上均超越现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有相机定位多为多阶段流水线，依赖构建、存储和维护成本高昂的专用复杂地图；而结构化地图（以紧凑且表达力强的基元组织场景）通常易于获得，蕴含丰富的结构与语义线索，但因地图基元与视觉观测间存在显著模态鸿沟，其用于直接定位的潜力尚未被充分挖掘，故提出通过将查询观测落地到地图基元来估计相机位姿的新思路。

Method: 以平面基元为实例化对象，设计PGGT网络：输入单张查询图像和一组平面地图基元，训练网络预测多个定位目标，从而以前馈方式完成6-DoF相机定位，并具备对未见环境的泛化能力；架构简单且可扩展。

Result: 在稠密表面地图和极简布局模型两类场景下均将定位性能推进至新的最先进水平。

Conclusion: 将观测与地图基元直接对齐的定位范式可行且有效，可摆脱昂贵复杂地图的依赖，并为可扩展的相机定位提供新方向。

Abstract: Camera localization methods are mostly multi-stage pipelines relying on dedicated and complex maps that are costly to construct, store, and maintain over time. Alternatively, well-structured maps that organize scenes with compact yet expressive primitives are often readily available, providing rich structural and semantic cues that humans can interpret. However, their potential for direct camera localization remains largely unexplored due to the significant modality gap between map primitives and visual observations. This motivates a new concept for localization, which estimates camera poses by grounding query observations to map primitives. In this paper, we instantiate the concept in the context of planar primitives with PGGT, the Primitive-Grounded Geometry Transformer network. Given a single query image and a set of planar map primitives, the network is trained to predict multiple localization targets for feed-forward 6-DoF camera localization that generalizes to unseen environments. With this straightforward and scalable architecture, our method pushes the state-of-the-art localization performance on both dense surface maps and minimal layout models.
