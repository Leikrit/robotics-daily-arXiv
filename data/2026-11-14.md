### [1] [A physics-constrained deep reinforcement learning method for autonomous mission planning of Titan deep space probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15389)
*Hanmin Li,Tao Wang,Shuangliang Liu,Zhe Zhang,Maodeng Li*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 提出PCM-PPO方法，用于土卫六深空探测器的自主任务规划，通过物理约束和掩码机制提高规划可行性与适应性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 土卫六探测距离远、环境复杂，地面闭环规划不可行；现有面向地球观测的规划方法泛化性和适应性不足。

Method: 建立土卫六离散时间马尔可夫决策过程，结合中心引力、J2摄动、第三体引力和下行遮挡等动力学与几何模型；采用固定容量目标池和状态矩阵表示任务状态；设计物理约束动作掩码构建可行决策域；采用集成训练策略优化策略。

Result: 对比、消融和不确定性实验验证了PCM-PPO的有效性。

Conclusion: PCM-PPO为土卫六深空探测器自主任务规划提供了可行且可解释的方案。

Abstract: Titan is a compelling target for future deep space exploration, while its extreme Earth-Titan distance and complex environment make continuous ground-in-the-loop planning impractical. Existing planning methods, primarily developed for Earth-observation scenarios, have limited generalizability to uncertain deep space conditions and adaptability to time-varying mission states. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a physics-constrained maskable proximal policy optimization (PCM-PPO) method for Titan autonomous mission planning. A Titan-specific discrete-time Markov decision process is established to characterize mission feasibility and resource constraints. A system dynamics and geometric model is incorporated to enhance physical representation, including central gravity, J2 perturbation, third-body attraction, and downlink occultation. To accommodate opportunity targets under a bounded action dimension, PCM-PPO organizes predefined and opportunity targets through a fixed-capacity target pool and represents the mission state with a target-wise state matrix and a global state vector. A physics-constrained action mask constructs an admissible decision domain from current mission states, guiding policy learning toward physically executable decisions and avoiding infeasible exploration. An integrated training strategy facilitates policy optimization under coupled constraints. Comparative, ablation, and uncertainty experiments validate the effectiveness of PCM-PPO. PCM-PPO provides a feasible and interpretable scheme for autonomous mission planning of Titan deep space probes.

### [2] [Spatial Foundry: A Multimodal Dataset for Spatial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17318)
*Ethan Lin,Yang Song*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出Spatial Foundry：5,280个带家具的住宅3D场景配置数据集，统一对齐视觉、几何与时间数据，支持空间智能研究。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 图像仅隐式表达空间关系，而孤立标注难以在同一参考系下研究外观、相机运动与场景变化，因此需要一个共享场景坐标、多模态对齐的数据集。

Method: 构建5,280个可编辑资产的住宅3D场景，提供平面图、透视与全景RGB、度量深度、点云、网格、实例级3D包围盒以及与视频配对的相机轨迹；共享场景坐标连接跨视角与跨时间观测，可编辑源场景支持生成新相机路径与受控物体变化；并描述数据表示与构建流程。

Result: 数据集覆盖生成、重建、感知、视频生成、世界建模与场景编辑等应用，受控的相机与场景变化可支撑空间感知与世界模型研究；公开项目页提供视频与交互式彩色点云预览。

Conclusion: Spatial Foundry通过多模态对齐与可编辑场景，为空间智能和世界模型研究提供统一数据基础。

Abstract: Spatial intelligence requires connecting visual observations to the geometry, objects, and persistent state of an environment. Images alone leave these relationships implicit, while disconnected annotations make it difficult to study appearance, camera motion, and scene changes under a common reference. We introduce Spatial Foundry, a collection of 5,280 furnished residential 3D scene configurations with editable assets and aligned visual, geometric, and temporal data. Its modalities include floor plans, perspective and panoramic RGB, metric depth, point clouds, meshes, instance-level 3D bounding boxes, and camera trajectories paired with video. Shared scene coordinates link observations across views and time; editable source scenes enable additional camera paths and controlled object changes. We describe the data representation and construction workflow, and discuss applications in floor-plan-conditioned 3D generation, geometric reconstruction, instance-level perception, camera-conditioned video generation, shared world modeling, and scene editing. Controlled camera and scene variations support spatial perception and world-model research. The public project page provides videos and an interactive colored point-cloud preview.

### [3] [WorldDraft: If Code Builds the World, Can Video Models Just Render It?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16217)
*Zhiyu Hou,Wen-Jie Shu,Harold Haodong Chen,Weilin Ruan,Chenfei Liao,Liuzhou Zhang,Disen Lan,Xiaogang Xu,Qi Chen,Ser-Nam Lim*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 论文提出WorldDraft基准（432个严格配对测试用例、48个可执行4D世界族、6类动态挑战）评估多模态编码智能体场景下视频模型作为生成式渲染器的能力，并发现世界保持与视觉实现存在张力、更丰富草稿损害外观保真、冲突线索引发最高64%源泄漏，为此提出即插即用的WorldDraft-Primer视觉提示策略。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多模态编码智能体将4D世界构建与视觉生成解耦，使视频模型从世界生成器转为生成式渲染器；核心问题是代码构建世界后视频模型能否仅负责渲染，而可靠的生成式渲染器需同时满足世界保持（保留规定的4D运动学与相机路径）和视觉实现（随时间持续绑定文本指定的目标属性），因此需要系统评估这一能力。

Method: 构建WorldDraft基准，包含48个可执行4D世界族、6类动态挑战类别下432个严格配对测试用例；采用3×3因子设计，在相同动态下正交组合草稿粒度（从抽象代理到预演previs）与外观对齐程度（从中性到冲突）；对12个代表性模型进行广泛评测，并提出即插即用的视觉提示策略WorldDraft-Primer。

Result: 评测揭示三点：(I) 世界保持与视觉实现在实际操作中呈明显张力；(II) 更丰富的草稿支架能锚定结构，但反而损害外观保真度；(III) 冲突的源线索触发严重源泄漏（最高达64%），暴露跨模态解耦的脆弱性。WorldDraft-Primer在冻结视频渲染器上持续改善两个目标。

Conclusion: WorldDraft与WorldDraft-Primer为连接程序化4D仿真与视频生成奠定基础，推动物理落地的生成式渲染发展。

Abstract: Multimodal coding agents decouple 4D world construction from visual generation, repurposing video models from world generators toward generative renderers. This division of labor raises a fundamental question: if code builds the world, can video models just render it? A reliable generative renderer hinges on two complementary criteria: (i) world preservation, retaining prescribed 4D kinematics and camera paths; and (ii) visual realization, persistently binding text-specified target attributes over time. To evaluate this capability, we introduce WorldDraft, a benchmark of $432$ strictly paired test cases across $48$ executable 4D world families spanning six dynamic challenge categories. Under a $3 \times 3$ factorial design, WorldDraft orthogonally crosses draft granularity (abstract proxy to previs) and appearance alignment (neutral to conflicting) under identical dynamics. Extensive evaluations across $12$ representative models reveal that: (I) world preservation and visual realization remain in stark operational tension; (II) richer draft scaffolding anchors structure but paradoxically impairs appearance fidelity; and (III) conflicting source cues trigger severe source leakage (up to $64\%$), exposing fragile cross-modal disentanglement. To mitigate these bottlenecks, we propose WorldDraft-Primer, a plug-and-play visual prompting strategy that consistently improves both objectives across frozen video renderers. Together, WorldDraft and WorldDraft-Primer lay a foundation for bridging programmatic 4D simulation with video generation, advancing physically grounded generative rendering.

</details>
