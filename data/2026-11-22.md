### [1] [Training Vision Models for LLM-Driven Weight-Space Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26408)
*Timo Ropinski,Sebastian Kranz*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个LLM驱动的视觉模型权重空间编程基准，包含超2600次编辑过程，并对比了标准训练、标签平滑、梯度可编辑训练与新型Edit-Ready Training（ERT）的效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 已有研究表明大语言模型可在某些设置下执行权重空间编程直接修改神经网络权重，但专门训练这些网络如何促进权重空间编程的成功尚不清楚。

Method: 在Waterbirds、CIFAR-100、PACS和Entity-30的重标记与纠错任务上，用五个LLM编辑卷积网络和视觉Transformer，构建LLM驱动权重空间编程基准；对每次编辑在留出图像上评估成功性、副作用与精度损失；比较标准训练、标签平滑、梯度可编辑训练与所提ERT（预演LLM执行的直接闭式权重编辑）。

Result: 共报告2600余次编辑过程；为编辑准备网络在标准网络难以编辑的情况下显著提升LLM编辑成功率；ERT在全部评估基线中为GPT-6 Astra和Claude Opus 5.5取得最高平均分。

Conclusion: 专门为编辑准备的训练可显著提升LLM权重空间编程的成功率，ERT是其中表现最佳的方法，并将发布LLM测试框架、任务、编辑轨迹与评分。

Abstract: It has been demonstrated that, in certain settings, large language models can perform weight-space programming to directly modify the weights of neural networks. However, it is unknown how dedicated training of these networks can contribute to the successes of weight-space programming. We present a benchmark of LLM-driven weight-space programming on vision models, in which five LLMs edit convolutional networks and vision transformers on relabeling and error-correction tasks on Waterbirds, CIFAR-100, PACS and Entity-30. We report more than 2,600 such edit processes, whereby each edit is scored on held-out images for success, side effects and accuracy loss. We compare standard training with label smoothing, gradient-based editable training, and our Edit-Ready Training (ERT), a novel method that rehearses the direct, closed-form weight edits LLMs perform. We show that preparing networks for editing raises the success of LLM edits substantially where standard networks are hard to edit. ERT further achieves the highest mean score for GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 across all evaluated baselines. We will release our LLM testing framework alongside all tasks, editing trajectories and scores. Project page: this https URL

### [2] [SceneAnything: Agentic Reconstruction of Large-Scale Mesh Scenes from a Single Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25340)
*Bowen Xue,Saeed Hadadan,Fabrice Rousselle,Zahra Montazeri,Ravi Ramamoorthi,Milos Hasan*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: SceneAnything是一个自动化系统，通过协调智能体、预训练视觉模型和图形工具，从真实和生成视频中重建可编辑的3D网格场景。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 直接由智能体编写场景构建程序进行重建常出现布局、尺度和光照错误，需要一种更可靠的自动化方法。

Method: 采用软件框架通过显式重建协议协调智能体；共享相机、深度和分割估计为组件构建提供基础；场景所有者智能体规划环境并指导作者编写参数化Blender程序；基于可见性和拟合的细节层次规则选择构建级别；未观察区域被补全并标记为推断；组装场景使用拟合光照和支持运动；修订经过程序化检查和独立视觉比较；接受的程序和经验可跨场景复用。

Result: 在100个场景上评估；子集比较显示，相比直接智能体重建，原始视图保真度提高；相比对比的视频到3D方法，新视图内容相似度更高。

Conclusion: SceneAnything能有效从视频重建可编辑3D场景，提升重建质量，并将发布框架、智能体指令、可复用库和场景。

Abstract: Reconstructing an editable 3D scene from video requires matching visible structure while plausibly completing unseen surfaces. Agents can write scene-construction programs, but direct reconstruction attempts often produce errors in layout, scale, and lighting. We introduce SceneAnything, an automated system for reconstructing mesh scenes from real and generated videos, enabling text-to-scene construction when paired with video generation. A software harness coordinates agents, pretrained vision models, and graphics tools through an explicit reconstruction protocol. Shared camera, depth, and segmentation estimates ground component construction: a scene-owner agent plans the environment and briefs authors, who write parameterized Blender programs. A visibility- and fit-based level-of-detail rule selects the construction level, while unseen regions are completed and marked as inferred. Assembled scenes use fitted lighting and supported motion, and revisions undergo programmatic checks and independent visual comparisons. Accepted programs and lessons are reused across scenes. We evaluate on 100 scenes; subset comparisons show improved original-view fidelity over direct-agent reconstruction and higher novel-view content similarity than the compared video-to-3D methods. We will release the harness, agent instructions, reusable libraries, and scenes.

</details>
