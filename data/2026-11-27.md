### [1] [Towards Closing the Performance Gap in Cross-Modal Place Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34877)
*Ofer Idan,Dan Badur,Yoli Shavit*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出CrossPlace两阶段跨模态地点识别框架，通过提高视觉token粒度、增强文本编码器能力和局部跨模态匹配，显著提升自然语言对地点的检索性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有视觉-语言模型在跨模态地点识别（CMPR）上远落后于视觉地点识别基线，作者假设主要瓶颈在于难以捕捉图像区域与文本细节间的细粒度对应关系。

Method: 两阶段框架：阶段一用联合对齐的DINOv2-B/14视觉主干与逐步微调的BGE-Large文本编码器做全局检索；阶段二用轻量级token级交叉注意力重排器精化候选。并系统分析各因素贡献。

Result: 视觉token密度带来最大性能提升，文本编码器容量与任务微调提供互补增益；在现有及新构建的城市规模、恶劣天气与光照基准上，Recall@1较先前跨模态方法提升两位数，缩小了与单模态文本检索及单模态VPR的差距。

Conclusion: CrossPlace有效缓解CMPR细粒度匹配瓶颈，验证了视觉token粒度是关键驱动因素，并在更真实苛刻条件下展现出稳健的跨模态地点识别能力。

Abstract: Cross-modal place recognition (CMPR) matches natural language descriptions against databases of geo-tagged reference images, enabling localization without visual queries. Despite its potential for language-guided navigation, existing vision-language models lag far behind visual place recognition (VPR) baselines, even after task adaptation. Unlike general-purpose text-to-image retrieval, place recognition relies on subtle local cues to distinguish visually similar locations. We therefore hypothesize that a primary bottleneck in CMPR is capturing fine-grained correspondences between image regions and text details, which requires higher visual token granularity, strong text representation capacity, and local cross-modal matching. To address this challenge, we propose CrossPlace, a two-stage CMPR framework. Stage 1 performs global retrieval by pairing a jointly aligned DINOv2-B/14 visual backbone with a progressively fine-tuned BGE-Large text encoder, while Stage 2 refines top candidates using a lightweight token-level cross-attention reranker. Through systematic analysis, we isolate the drivers of retrieval accuracy and show that visual token density provides the largest performance improvement, while text encoder capacity and task fine-tuning offer complementary gains. To evaluate robustness under realistic conditions, we also extend CMPR evaluation to city-scale and severe weather and illumination settings. Across all existing and new benchmarks, CrossPlace achieves double-digit Recall@1 improvements over prior cross-modal methods, closes the gap to unimodal text retrieval, and narrows it to unimodal VPR. ### [2] [VivaWorld: Long-Horizon Video World Model With Compositional 3D Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33591)
*Ying Jiang,Donglai Xiang,Anita Hu,Masha Shugrina*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: VivaWorld 提出一种从单张图像生成几何与视角一致长视频的框架，通过混合3D表示和混合相机嵌入实现精准相机控制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 长时视频生成在交互式世界建模、物理智能与空间推理中至关重要，但缺乏3D基础时难以保持几何一致性和准确的相机控制。

Method: 构建组合式混合3D表示：结合保留观测场景几何与外观的重建点云，以及提供360°新视角完整物体几何的生成纹理网格；引入连通区域滤波器去除不一致漂浮组件以增强跨视角重建鲁棒性；提出结合全局与物体锚定Plücker射线的混合相机嵌入，捕捉场景级相机运动和相机-物体关系，并通过控制适配器与视觉引导一起注入视频生成模型。

Result: 大量实验表明VivaWorld在视觉质量和相机控制上达到最先进水平，支持可控新视角合成、长时场景探索和物理AI。

Conclusion: VivaWorld通过3D基础与混合相机嵌入有效解决了长时视频生成中的几何一致性和相机控制难题，为交互式世界建模和物理AI提供了有力支持。

Abstract: Long-horizon video generation is key to interactive world modeling, physical intelligence, and spatial reasoning, yet maintaining geometric consistency and accurate camera control remains challenging without 3D grounding. We introduce \textbf{VivaWorld}, a framework for generating geometry- and view-consistent long videos from an image. We first construct a compositional hybrid 3D representation that combines reconstructed point clouds, which preserve observed scene geometry and appearance, with generated textured meshes, which provide complete object geometry across $360^\circ$ novel views. We also introduce a connected-region filter removing inconsistent floating components for robust cross-view reconstruction and propose a hybrid camera embedding that combines global and object-anchored Plücker rays to capture both scene-level camera motion and camera-object relationships, and inject it together with the visual guidance into a video generation model via a control adapter. Extensive experiments show VivaWorld achieves state-of-the-art visual quality and camera control, while supporting controllable novel-view synthesis, long-horizon scene exploration, and physical AI. ### [3] [LiDAR Intensity Reconstruction with Limited Real Data: An Architecture Benchmark under Fixed-Epoch Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34724)
*Christopher M. Coovrey,Aniruddha Bora*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 该论文在固定20轮训练协议下，使用真实SemanticKITTI与合成VoxelScape距离图像数据，对九种神经网络架构从深度、语义标签、入射角和材质反射率重建LiDAR强度进行了基准测试，并评估了不同真实数据比例下的性能权衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 物理仿真可补充稀缺的真实LiDAR强度测量，但其有效性取决于学习架构和可用真实训练数据量，因此需要系统比较不同架构在不同真实数据比例下的重建表现。

Method: 选取九种神经架构，以深度、语义标签、入射角和材质反射率为输入重建LiDAR强度；所有模型统一训练20轮；报告MAE、MSE、PSNR、SSIM、推理时间和频谱能量一致性；并对四种架构测试0%、1%、5%、10%、20%、50%、100%真实帧的固定规模混合训练。

Result: 仅用真实数据训练时UNet-V2重建指标最佳（MAE 0.234、MSE 0.140、PSNR 8.64 dB、SSIM 0.647），其次是DDPM；在含1%–10%真实数据的多种混合配置中DDPM误差更低、图像质量指标更高，而100%真实数据时UNet-V2领先；频谱分析显示不同频段存在架构相关差异。

Conclusion: 这些发现刻画了所述数据混合与固定轮数协议下的重建权衡，但未确立算力匹配下的优越性，也未将扩散目标与骨干差异分离。

Abstract: Physics-derived simulation can supplement scarce real-world LiDAR intensity measurements, but its effectiveness depends on the learning architecture and the amount of real training data available. We benchmark nine neural architectures for LiDAR intensity reconstruction from depth, semantic labels, incidence angle, and material reflectance, using real SemanticKITTI and synthetic VoxelScape range-image data. All models follow a fixed 20-epoch protocol; this controls the number of training passes, not the computational cost across architectures. We report mean absolute error (MAE), mean squared error (MSE), peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR), structural similarity (SSIM), inference timing, and spectral-energy agreement. We also evaluate four selected architectures with fixed-size training mixtures containing 0\%, 1\%, 5\%, 10\%, 20\%, 50\%, or 100\% real frames. With real-only training, UNet-V2 achieves the best reported reconstruction metrics (MAE 0.234, MSE 0.140, PSNR 8.64 dB, and SSIM 0.647), followed by a denoising diffusion model (DDPM). In several mixtures containing 1--10\% real data, DDPM achieves lower error and higher image-quality metrics than the evaluated deterministic alternatives, whereas UNet-V2 leads with 100\% real data. Spectral analysis additionally reveals architecture-dependent discrepancies across frequency bands. These findings characterize reconstruction trade-offs under the stated data mixtures and fixed-epoch protocol, without establishing compute-matched superiority or isolating the diffusion objective from backbone differences.

### [4] [ProManO: Beyond Sim(3) for Metric Upgrade of Feed-Forward Geometry with Visual-Inertial Anchors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34480)
*Lingxiang Hu,Zhenjun Zhao,Qi Shao,Naima Ait Oufroukh,Fabien Bonardi,Raymond Ghandour,Shenghai Yuan,Javier Civera,Baoru Huang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出ProManO，在保持预标定内参不变的十维流形上优化前馈几何与VIO锚点的对齐，优于Sim(3)。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有将前馈几何升级为度量尺度的方法要么用相似变换（无法表达深度相关畸变），要么用投影变换（破坏预标定内参），缺少既保持内参又对齐稀疏VIO锚点的正确变换。

Method: 证明与预标定内参兼容的变换构成十维流形，其中超出Sim(3)的三自由度定位无穷远平面；提出ProManO概率流形优化框架，在单一EM过程中按沿射线不确定性和内点概率对每个锚点加权，仅在可观测处精化无穷远平面。

Result: 在EuRoC、CID-SIMS、Oxford Spires和KITTI-360上，ProManO取得最高平均F1，并在全部四个数据集上优于Sim(3)对齐。

Conclusion: 与内参兼容的十维流形中额外的三自由度只能由度量深度或多视图约束求解，ProManO可证明目标值不劣于最优相似对齐。

Abstract: Upgrading feed-forward geometry to metric scale with visual-inertial odometry (VIO) raises the question of which correction should align dense feed-forward prediction with sparse metric VIO anchors. Existing methods use either a similarity transformation, which cannot represent depth-dependent distortion, or a projective transformation, which alters pre-calibrated intrinsics. We prove that the transformations compatible with pre-calibrated intrinsics form a ten-dimensional manifold. Beyond Sim(3), this manifold contains three degrees of freedom (DoF) that locate the plane at infinity and leave residuals growing quadratically with depth range after any similarity alignment. These DoF are invisible in the image and can be resolved only by metric depth along viewing rays or by multi-view constraints. To estimate them, we propose ProManO, a probabilistic manifold optimization framework in which every anchor is weighted by its along-ray uncertainty and inlier probability within a single expectation-maximization (EM) procedure. ProManO certifies the similarity synchronization and refines the plane at infinity only where it is observable, which guarantees an objective value no worse than that of the optimal similarity alignment. Experiments on EuRoC, CID-SIMS, Oxford Spires, and KITTI-360 demonstrate that ProManO achieves the highest mean F1 and outperforms Sim(3) alignment on all four datasets. The project page is available at this https URL .

</details>
