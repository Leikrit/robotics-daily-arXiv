### [1] [A Multimodal Haptic Interface for Simultaneous Softness and Thermal Modulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08398)
*Joshua Brown,Fernando Bello,Lynette Jones*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 提出一种结合颗粒阻塞变刚度与冷热水热传递的多模态触觉显示技术，可同时控制柔软度与温度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有触觉显示器虽能单独模拟各类皮肤触觉线索，但缺乏能同时控制多种线索的设备。

Method: 基于颗粒阻塞实现刚度变化，利用加热和冷却水的热传递实现温度变化，构建原型并进行机械与热学评估。

Result: 刚度可控制提升300%；表面温度均匀，可实现10°C至50°C范围，闭环控制误差保持在0.2°C以内。

Conclusion: 该工作推进了多模态触觉显示器设计，可应用于感知研究、医学模拟和情感触觉等领域。

Abstract: Haptic interactions, such as touching an object in virtual reality, involve detecting a plethora of sensations through the skin. Whilst tactile displays exist to simulate and manipulate all of these cues individually, little progress has been made constructing devices that can control multiple cues simultaneously. This paper presents a tactile display technology that offers both softness and temperature change. A prototype display, based on particle jamming and the thermal transfer of heated and cooled water, is evaluated in terms of its ability to change both stiffness and temperature. Mechanical evaluations demonstrate controllable stiffness increase of 300%, whilst thermal characterisation showed strong surface uniformity and a range of achievable temperatures from 10°C up to 50°C, with closed-loop control maintaining a 0.2°C error from a setpoint. This work advances the design of multimodal tactile displays with applications to perceptual research, medical simulation, and affective haptics.

### [2] [Do sEMG Foundation Models Improve Cross-Subject Generalisation?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08199)
*William Raftery,Dario Farina*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 系统评估三个sEMG基础模型在三个手势分类数据集上的表现，发现预训练仅在会话内评估中带来提升，跨受试者评估时优势基本消失；受试者数量少的数据集上预训练几乎无益，经典机器学习基线可持平或超越基础模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有评估难以判断预训练本身是否足以缓解sEMG机器学习中严重的跨受试者过拟合问题，因此需要隔离预训练贡献进行系统评估。

Method: 在三个手势分类数据集上，采用会话内与跨受试者两种划分，并使用三种微调策略隔离预训练的作用，对三个sEMG基础模型进行系统评估。

Result: 当训练与测试样本来自同一记录会话时预训练提升性能；但在跨受试者评估下该收益基本消失。在受试者数量较少的数据集中，预训练几乎没有或完全没有收益，经典机器学习基线可达到或超过基础模型性能。

Conclusion: 预训练的收益高度依赖评估设置，跨受试者泛化仍是sEMG基础模型的关键瓶颈，且在小样本受试者场景下基础模型未必优于经典方法。

Abstract: sEMG Foundation Models aim to learn general purpose representations over diverse subjects and recording conditions that boost performance on a variety of downstream tasks. However, existing evaluations make it difficult to discern whether pretraining alone is sufficient to combat the cross-subject overfitting which makes machine learning on sEMG so challenging. We conduct a systematic evaluation of three sEMG foundation models across three gesture classification datasets under both intra-session and inter-subject splits, using three fine-tuning strategies designed to isolate the contribution of pretraining. We find that pretraining boosts performance when train and test samples are drawn from the same recording session, but this benefit largely disappears under inter-subject evaluation. In low subject count datasets, pretraining provides little to no benefit and classical ML baselines match or exceed foundation model performance.

### [3] [Structured Machine Theory of Mind from Agent Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07208)
*Nigel F. Doering,Rahath Malladi,Arshia Sangwan,M Tanjid Hasan Tonmoy,Jacob Doan,David Danks,Tauhidur Rahman*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 提出结构化机器心理理论SMToM框架，基于BDI因果结构从轨迹中显式归因信念与欲望，实现可解释的反事实干预。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大规模轨迹预测模型只优化统计精度，不表示驱动行为的心理状态，因此只能预测而无法回答信念或偏好改变时行为如何变化，缺乏原理性干预能力。

Method: SMToM在BDI因果结构下对信念和欲望进行独立监督的显式归因；核心是仅接收预测心理状态通道和当前轨迹嵌入的目标头，使反事实干预成为直接操作；在真值心理状态已知的受控行人导航域实例化为BDIBottleneck，并与仅轨迹、无监督上下文及匹配的辅助监督基线比较。

Result: BDIBottleneck优于仅轨迹和无监督上下文神经基线，与匹配的辅助监督上下文基线相当且提供可编辑心理状态接口；欲望反事实实验表明替换推断偏好为不同活动类型会一致地将预测目的地移向相关位置；信念反事实实验表明将某位置标记为不可用会降低其作为目的地的预测概率，但方式较复杂。

Conclusion: 在受控导航环境中，显式BDI结构化监督是纵向轨迹数据可解释与反事实分析的可行基础。

Abstract: Predictive models of human behavior trained on large-scale trajectory data optimize for statistical accuracy without representing the mental states that causally generate behavior. Such models support prediction but not principled intervention: they cannot answer how an agent's behavior would change if its beliefs or preferences were different. We introduce Structured Machine Theory of Mind (SMToM), a framework that attributes explicit, independently supervised belief and desire representations from observed trajectories within a Belief-Desire-Intention (BDI) causal structure. The central architectural element is a goal head that consumes only the predicted mental-state channels and a current-trajectory embedding; counterfactual intervention on beliefs and desires is a direct operation. We instantiate SMToM on a controlled pedestrian navigation domain where ground-truth mental states are known by construction, enabling evaluation of attribution accuracy, local channel interventions, and trajectory-level counterfactual diagnostics. The resulting model, BDIBottleneck, improves over trajectory-only and unsupervised-context neural baselines. It is also competitive with a matched auxiliary-supervised context baseline while providing an editable mental-state interface that the unbottlenecked baseline lacks. Counterfactual experiments on desires confirm that substituting an agent's inferred preferences with a different activity type coherently shifts predicted destinations toward relevant locations. Counterfactual experiments on beliefs confirm that marking a location as unavailable in the agent's belief state reduces its predicted probability as a destination, though in a complex way. These results demonstrate, in a controlled navigation setting, that explicit BDI-structured supervision is a viable foundation for interpretable and counterfactual analysis of longitudinal trajectory data.

### [4] [EgoGeneralist: Unifying Egocentric Action-Video Tasks through Video-Native Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08477)
*Yanlin Jin,Bowen Yang,Cheng Zhang,Yifan Yang,Zhe Li,Jianfei Cai,Kai Zhu*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: EgoGeneralist将预训练视频生成器适配为统一的第一人称动作条件视频生成、姿态估计与联合动作-视频生成的通用框架。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代视频模型具有强视觉先验，但原生不表示度量3D空间中的人体动作，作者希望桥接预训练视觉知识与度量3D人体运动。

Method: 提出角色自适应动作渲染器，为多方向动作-视频学习提供视频原生表示，使度量运动既可作为空间控制信号又可作为可恢复的生成目标；并通过轻量适配实现跨流动作-视频耦合，在保留预训练视觉先验的同时建立度量运动与视觉动态的紧密对应。

Result: 该框架覆盖五类动作-视频任务；尽管仅在受控实验室录像上适配，却能泛化到多样未见场景与交互，具备有效的动作控制和视觉质量。

Conclusion: EgoGeneralist以轻量适配统一了动作与视频的表征与生成，成功连接预训练视觉先验与度量3D人体运动，展现出良好的泛化能力。

Abstract: Modern video models possess strong visual priors, yet do not natively represent human action in metric 3D space. We present EgoGeneralist, which adapts pretrained video generators to unify action-conditioned egocentric video generation, pose estimation, and joint action-video generation. Our role-adaptive action renderer provides video-native representations for multidirectional action-video learning, making metric motion usable as both a spatial control signal and a recoverable generation target. Cross-stream action-video coupling establishes close correspondence between metric motion and visual dynamics through lightweight adaptation while preserving the pretrained visual prior. Together, these designs establish a generalist framework spanning five action-video tasks. Despite adaptation only on controlled laboratory recordings, EgoGeneralist generalizes to diverse unseen scenes and interactions with effective action control and visual quality, bridging pretrained visual knowledge and metric 3D human motion.

### [5] [InstantHand: Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting for Animatable Hand Avatar Reconstruction from a Single Image](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08330)
*Chanho Kim,Muhammad Salman Ali,Kyeonghwan Gwak,Muhammad Shaheryar,Incheol Park,Jeongwan On,Seungryul Baek*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: InstantHand 是一种前馈框架，仅需单张图像即可构建可动画的 3D 高斯手部化身，无需测试时优化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有手部化身重建依赖多视图捕捉或逐主体优化，可扩展性差。

Method: 利用现成 MANO、相机与掩码估计，从单张图像前馈构建 3D 高斯手部化身；提出 Soft Alpha Band 正则化器聚焦轮廓边界监督，以及 Reliability-gated Appearance Transfer 按渲染贡献迁移源颜色并补全未观测区域。

Result: 构建化身耗时 2.84 秒，渲染速度 161 FPS，在 InterHand2.6M 上匹配逐主体优化基线，在 HanCo 零样本评估中具竞争力。

Conclusion: InstantHand 实现了高效、可扩展的单图手部化身重建，性能媲美优化方法并保持实时渲染。

Abstract: Hand avatar reconstruction typically relies on multi-view capture or per-subject optimization, limiting its scalability. We introduce InstantHand, a feed-forward framework that constructs an animatable 3D Gaussian hand avatar from a single image using off-the-shelf MANO, camera, and mask estimates, without test-time optimization. Image-averaged losses provide insufficient supervision for alpha leakage, which thickens thin finger contours. To address this issue, we propose a Soft Alpha Band regularizer that concentrates supervision on silhouette boundaries. Furthermore, colors regressed from image features can drift toward a learned prior even in observed regions. Our Reliability-gated Appearance Transfer preserves observed appearance by transferring source colors according to each Gaussian's rendering contribution, while completing unobserved regions using the learned prior conditioned on global source identity. InstantHand constructs an avatar in 2.84 seconds and renders at 161 FPS, matching per-subject-optimized baselines on InterHand2.6M and remaining competitive in zero-shot evaluation on HanCo.

### [6] [4D Point Splatting for mmWave Radar Micro-Doppler Differentiable Rendering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08342)
*Adnan Armouti,Guanyue Qian,Rajalakshmi Nandakumar*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出4D点溅射（4DPS），一种基于物理的可微渲染器，能将显式运动场景拟合到真实毫米波雷达采集，生成原始复数回波以训练下游模型，显著缩小仿真到现实的差距。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 毫米波雷达人体感知依赖下游模型，但雷达数据集规模远落后于视觉和语言，暴力采集无法弥补；现有合成方法（前向模拟器、学习生成器、静态场景逆渲染）无法拟合运动人体的真实采集，存在仿真到现实差距。

Method: 用同步相机初始化房间和人体，将场景表示为携带材质、法向和速度的有向点，将每个点溅射到距离和多普勒 bin，输出真实雷达记录的原始复数回波；对渲染数据应用相同信号处理获得任意表示以训练SOTA下游模型；并实现自定义CUDA内核。

Result: 在HuPR和RT-Pose上分别拟合235和100个序列，距离-方位角中位相关0.940和0.830，距离-多普勒0.829和0.695，微多普勒0.988和0.966；RT-Pose上仅用渲染数据训练的姿势模型在真实数据达21.9 cm MPJPE，真实训练为16.7 cm，RF-Genesis基线为127.6 cm；单张H100上拟合一个序列分别耗时5.7和12.9分钟。

Conclusion: 4DPS首次实现毫米波雷达数据集规模的逆渲染，重建和下游仿真到现实差距均达SOTA，可支持任何以人为中心的下游任务与模型的合成数据生成及场景编辑数据增强。

Abstract: Millimeter-wave radar resolves the micrometer displacements of a moving body as micro-Doppler, enabling gait, gesture, activity, pose and vital-sign sensing. Each task relies on a downstream model trained on real data, yet radar datasets lag orders of magnitude behind vision and language. Brute-force collection will not close that gap, and existing synthesis methods (forward-only simulators, learned generators and static-scene inverse renderers) cannot fit a real capture of a moving body, leaving a sim2real gap. We present 4D Point Splatting (4DPS), a physics-based differentiable renderer that fits an explicit moving scene to a real capture. Using the synchronized camera to initialize the room and body, we represent the scene as oriented points carrying material, normal and velocity, and splat each point into range and Doppler bins. Our output is the raw complex return a real radar records, so we apply the same signal processing on the rendered data to obtain any representation for training any SOTA downstream model. We fit 235 sequences on HuPR and 100 on RT-Pose, two datasets with different radars. Our reconstructions set the state of the art, with median correlations of 0.940 and 0.830 on range-azimuth, 0.829 and 0.695 on range-Doppler, and 0.988 and 0.966 on micro-Doppler. Our renders also set the state of the art in downstream sim2real gap. On RT-Pose, a pose model trained on them reaches 21.9 cm MPJPE on real data, against 16.7 cm for real training and 127.6 cm for the RF-Genesis baseline, where both the rendered- and real-trained models learn nearly the same features. Our custom CUDA kernels fit a sequence in 5.7 minutes on HuPR and 12.9 on RT-Pose on one H100, making dataset-scale inverse rendering of mmWave radar possible for the first time. This work can enable synthetic data generation for any human-centered downstream task and model, and scene editing for data augmentation.

</details>
